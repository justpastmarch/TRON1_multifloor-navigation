# Research Brief

Core question: Is lateral-offset and heading closed-loop correction the best stair-driving approach for TRON1, given its current code, recorded sensor data, IMU/odometry error, structural simplicity, safety, and mobility requirements?

Deliverables: Korean PDF, DOCX, and Markdown.

Constraints: Read-only research. Do not move the robot, publish commands, change runtime state, or modify production code/configuration.

Axes:

1. Current stair controller and invariants.
2. Recorded bag and evidence quality.
3. IMU and wheel-odometry error under stair dynamics.
4. Vision and 2D/3D LiDAR stair-relative perception.
5. Control architectures and stability.
6. Simpler alternatives and failure modes.
7. Verification experiments and acceptance criteria.
